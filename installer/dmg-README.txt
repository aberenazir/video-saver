THUMBSTUDIO VIDEO SAVER

1. Drag "ThumbStudio Video Saver" onto the Applications folder next to it.
2. Open Applications and double-click ThumbStudio Video Saver.

The first time, the Mac may say it can't check the app for malicious software (it isn't from the App Store).
Then: click Done, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to "ThumbStudio Video Saver was blocked" (it may say "Video Saver")
and click Open Anyway (then Open). You only do this once.

Videos are saved to Downloads > Video Saver unless you pick another folder in the app.
For Instagram stories or private videos: Settings > "Use my login from" the browser you're logged in with.
